⏳ Past Continuous

dire cosa stavi facendo — I was reading at 8 o'clock

💡 La regola

Il Past Continuous dice cosa stava succedendo in un momento del passato: un'azione in corso, non ancora finita. Si forma sempre così:

was / were  +  verbo‑ing
Soggettowas / wereEsempio
IwasI was reading.
YouwereYou were singing.
He / She / ItwasShe was sleeping.
WewereWe were playing.
TheywereThey were watching TV.
🎨 Trucco: was = per UNO (I, he, she, it) · were = per PIÙ di uno (you, we, they). E non dimenticare mai la ‑ing! 🧲

🗣️ Frasi pronte (ascolta!)

IngleseItaliano🔊
I was reading a book.Stavo leggendo un libro.
She was sleeping.Stava dormendo.
We were playing football.Stavamo giocando a calcio.
I wasn't listening.Non stavo ascoltando.
Were you studying?Stavi studiando?
While I was cooking, she was reading.Mentre cucinavo, lei leggeva.
❌ Negativo: was/were + not + ‑ing → I wasn't sleeping.
❓ Domanda: Was/Were + soggetto + ‑ing? → Were you studying?
🔗 while = mentre · when = quando (due azioni nel passato).
