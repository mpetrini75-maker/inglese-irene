✅ Forma affermativa

PronomeVerbo (es. work)Ascolta
Iwork
Youwork
Heworks
Sheworks
Itworks
Wework
Theywork
🎯 Regola: I/You/We/They → verbo base · He/She/It → verbo + s

⚠️ Casi speciali della -s

-o / -ss / -sh / -ch / -x → +es
go → goes
watch → watches
wash → washes
consonante + y → ies
study → studies
fly → flies
carry → carries
💡 Eccezione: have → has (non "haves"!)

❌ Forma negativa

PronomeNegazione + verboAscolta
I / You / We / Theydon't + verbo base
He / She / Itdoesn't + verbo base
⚠️ Attenzione! Con doesn't il verbo torna alla forma base — senza la -s!
✗ She doesn't works    ✓ She doesn't work

❓ Forma interrogativa

PronomeStrutturaEsempioAscolta
I / You / We / TheyDo + sogg. + verbo?Do you work?
He / She / ItDoes + sogg. + verbo?Does she work?
💡 Anche qui, con Does il verbo resta senza -s:
✗ Does she works?    ✓ Does she work?
